3. Commission Structure

  • Tier 1 (Realtor Pro+): $750 flat fee per closed project at the Tier 1 package level.
  • Tier 2 (Core / Event Venue 360): 10â15% of net project revenue, confirmed in writing at the proposal stage prior to project start. Standard rate is 10%; partners with 3+ closed referrals qualify for 15% upon written confirmation from RSD.
