  • What has this problem already cost you?
  • What happens if nothing changes in 6 months?
  • Where are you losing deals you should be winning?

  • Who is your best current client? What made them say yes?
  • What does your ideal client Google before hiring?
  • What proof matters most to them: portfolio, reviews, referrals?

  • Where does your best client spend their attention?
  • What channel has produced your best conversion rate?
  • What's the fastest path from first touch to discovery call?
E

How does one client become ten?

Every engagement should have a built-in expansion engine. Referrals, case studies, affiliate media, upsell pathways. Design the first deal so that it generates the next three â turning clients into distribution assets.

  • What would make this client refer two more like them?
