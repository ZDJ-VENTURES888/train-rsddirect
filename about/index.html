Sales Method

The GRAVITY Framework

Every conversation follows a behavioral sequence designed to reach the truth fast — and present the right solution to the right business at the right time.

GGround
RRead
AAddress
VValidate
IInstall
TTest
YYield
G — Ground
"How much does one missed lead cost you in a typical month?"
We anchor the conversation in real numbers before we mention a solution. You can't solve a problem you haven't measured.
R — Read
"Is the problem visibility, trust, or conversion — or all three?"
We diagnose before we prescribe. Visibility, trust, and conversion are different problems that require different fixes.
A — Address
"Your work is clearly exceptional. So what would it mean if the business finally reflected that?"
We meet you exactly where you are — validating the craft, then naming the gap the system hasn't filled.
V — Validate
"So what you're saying is — the work is there, the reputation is there, but the pipeline doesn't reflect it."
We mirror back what we heard. You confirm. That moment of agreement is the real starting point.
I — Install
"Here's exactly what this system would look like built around your business specifically."
Not a template. A connected system mapped directly to what you just confirmed you're losing.
T — Test
"Are you open to a 20-minute walkthrough mapped to your last three projects?"
One soft ask. One next step. No pressure, no manufactured urgency. The work speaks for itself.
