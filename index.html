The Physics Principle Behind the Metaphor

Gravity doesn't push. It pulls — invisibly, inevitably, proportionally. The biggest mistake in sales is the push model: convincing, pitching, overcoming resistance. Top performers don't push. They create conditions where the prospect moves toward the close the way objects move toward mass — because the environment makes it natural. Every element of GRAVITY increases your pull. The prospect doesn't feel persuaded. They feel drawn. That's the difference between a close that sticks and one that reverses.

Behavioral Foundation

The Ego Triangle

Every objection, hesitation, and power play traces back to one or more corners of this triangle. Diagnose the threat. Address the corner. The logic handles itself.

Identity — Who I Believe I Am

"This is not who I am. Do not threaten that."

  • Self-ConceptBehavior doesn't match self-image. They get defensive when exposed.
  • Narrative"You're ruining my story." Internal scripts: I'm smart, I'm always right, I'm a victim.
  • ReputationDon't make me look bad. Social version of identity — embarrassment in front of others.
  • Victimhood"I am the one who was wronged." Moral superiority defense.
  • SignificanceIf unseen or unacknowledged, they create drama to reassert presence.
Trigger Phrases to Recognize

"Don't talk to me like that." · "That's not what I meant." · "I am NOT the bad guy here." · "How dare you accuse me of that." · "You don't know who you're talking to."

Control — My Agency & Power

"I need to feel like I have power and predictability."

  • Autonomy"Don't tell me what to do." Even polite direction can feel like a power grab.
  • Frame Hijack"Don't rewrite what just happened." If their version of reality is overruled, they resist hard.
  • Public Power"You made me look like I'm not in charge here." Social dominance disruption.
  • CarryoverNo power elsewhere in life → overcorrecting here. Road rage, entitlement, refusal to yield.
  • Uncertainty"I can't predict what's next." Brain hates unpredictability — especially with ego attached.
Trigger Phrases to Recognize

"You're not listening to me." · "You're twisting my words." · "I don't have to take this." · "Let me finish." · "You don't get to decide that."

Safety — My Status & Belonging

"I need to feel accepted, valued, and not excluded."

  • Social StatusFear of being seen as less-than. Loss of standing in their peer group is a threat.
  • Belonging"Am I still part of the tribe?" Exclusion triggers deep fear — even in business contexts.
  • RejectionWhen their ideas aren't validated, the brain reads it as social exclusion.
  • Loss AversionPerceived loss of status or advantage triggers defensive behavior disproportionate to the actual risk.
  • Shame"What will others think?" The intersection of Identity and Safety — the most paralyzing combination.
Trigger Phrases to Recognize

"I can't believe you'd say that." · "Everyone else agrees with me." · "I'm not stupid." · "That's embarrassing." · "What are people going to think?"

GRAVITY — Course Architecture
The unified operating principle · All 7 forces mapped

GRAVITY is the retrieval system that makes the entire framework accessible under pressure. Seven letters. Seven forces. Every module maps to one letter — so when you're in a live conversation and something feels off, you can locate which force is missing and correct in real time.

The physics metaphor is not decorative. Gravity doesn't push — it creates conditions where movement toward you is the path of least resistance. Your goal in every conversation is not to convince. It is to make not-buying feel more effortful than buying.

G — Ground Authority R — Read the Room A — Architect the Open V — Validate Reality I — Isolate Outcome T — Trigger Close Y — Your System
01
GRAVITY Force G — Ground Your Authority
ACSS + FATE + PCP
Chase Hughes · NCI University

The ACSS Priority Stack governs every human interaction before any script activates. The most important insight: 90% of entrepreneurs start at the bottom of the stack (Skills). The asymmetric advantage is always starting at the top.

  • A — Authority: Behavioral composure, pace control, physical stillness. Perceived in the first three seconds before credentials matter. Slow speech. Long pauses. No filler words.
  • C — Comfort: Your internal state in live interaction. Can you hold space without shrinking? Trained through the Composure Protocol: five minutes before every call.
  • S — Social Skills: Rapport, warmth, genuine curiosity. Use their exact words back to create unconscious familiarity.
  • S — Skills: Scripts, frameworks, techniques. The last priority — only work when A, C, and first S are in place.

FATE Model: Four brain override switches — Focus via Novelty, Authority via Composure, Tribe via Social Proof, Emotion as Memory.

PCP Cascade: Perception → Context → Persuasion. In that order, always. Skip P and C and you get resistance.

"The brain cannot ignore novelty. Before I get into anything — I noticed something about your booking process that I think you'd actually want to know about."FATE: Focus Activation
02
GRAVITY Force R — Read the Room
Statement → Question Flow
CIA Elicitation · NCI University

The most underused technique in sales. When you ask a question first, defenses go up. When you make a statement first — then follow with a question — the brain's screening mechanism has already been bypassed.

Make a slightly incomplete or mildly inaccurate observation. Their natural drive to be understood compels them to provide the correction. Follow with a question that locks in the intel.

  • Deliberate Understatement: "I imagine your leads are coming in pretty consistently right now." → They correct you → real picture revealed.
  • Provocative Undervalue: "Honestly, most places at your level probably don't need a 3D tour." → They argue they DO need it — doing your pitch for you.
  • The Lego Drop: Two facts. No conclusion. Three seconds of silence. Their brain connects them. Their conclusion is irresistible because it's theirs.
  • Black Swan Probe: "It sounds like there's something else going on beyond just the budget question." → Surfaces the hidden constraint that controls the decision.
"Statements reveal. Questions defend. The sequence that bypasses every wall is: observe it, state it, then invite the correction. What they correct tells you everything." — Chase Hughes / NCI University
03
GRAVITY Force A — Architect the Opening
The 90-Second Brain Capture
Five-step sequence · Not improvisation — execution

The first 90 seconds wire the operating system that runs every word afterward. Five precision moves. Get this right and the rest of the conversation becomes inevitable.

  • 0:00–0:10 — Novelty Hook: "Before I get into anything — I noticed something about your booking process that I think you'd actually want to know about."
  • 0:10–0:30 — Perception Reframe: "I'd rather spend this conversation understanding your situation than pitching you anything. Does that work?"
  • 0:30–0:55 — Micro-Identity Agreement: "Are you the kind of owner who thinks about the experience a guest has before they ever actually show up?"
  • 0:55–1:20 — The Lego Drop: "Properties with immersive virtual tours hold prospect attention five times longer. [pause] You mentioned your leads go cold after that first inquiry." [3-second silence. Say nothing.]
  • 1:20–1:30 — Frame Lock: "I want to ask you a few questions about how bookings actually work in your world right now, then I'll tell you honestly whether what we do is even relevant to you. Sound fair?"
04
GRAVITY Force V — Validate Their Reality
Voss LABEL + MIRROR System
FBI Negotiation Methodology

When you name their emotion before they do, the wall comes down. When you mirror and hold silence, intelligence flows freely.

  • L — Listen for emotion below the words: Not content — feeling. Frustration, fear, pride, urgency, doubt. The emotion drives the objection, not the logic.
  • A — Acknowledge without agreeing: "It seems like…" / "It sounds like…" / "It looks like…" Recognition is not agreement.
  • B — Accusation Audit: Name every negative thing they might be thinking. "You might be thinking this is just another agency pitch. That's fair."
  • E — Elicit "That's Right": "You're right" signals dismissal. "That's right" signals genuine alignment. Summarize their world completely until you hear it.
  • L — Let "No" Work For You: "No" creates safety. People honor decisions protected with a no. "Is now a bad time?" — a no here is permission to proceed.
"Help me understand — what would need to be true for this to be an easy yes for you?"The Black Swan Question — surfaces hidden constraints that control the deal
05
GRAVITY Force T — Trigger the Close
Tipping Point Detection System
Six signals · One pivot protocol · Three-move close

You don't create tipping points. You read them and catch the wave at the right moment.

  • Blink Rate Drop: Eyes slow. Sustained contact. Absorbed expression. → Slow pace. Hold silence. Move to proposal language.
  • Lean Forward: Physical movement toward you. Arms uncross. Nodding. → Reciprocate warmth. Narrow to specifics.
  • Future-Tense Language: "So when we do this…" / "What would the timeline look like?" → Match their language. Mirror the future frame.
  • Ownership Questions: "Can we customize that?" / "Do you do payment plans?" → Buying signals. Answer fully. Confirm next step.
  • Social Proof Request: "Have you worked with anyone like us?" → Deliver Fleur De Lis case study immediately.
  • Processing Silence: Strong statement. They go quiet. Eyes shift inward. → Do NOT fill silence. Wait for yes or real objection.
"Are you open to me putting together something specific to your situation so you can see what this looks like solved for your space?"Move 03 — Soft Commitment Ask · "Are you open to" eliminates resistance
06
GRAVITY Force I — Isolate the Dream Outcome
Hormozi Value Engineering
$100M Value Equation · DPP Sequence · MESO

The Value Equation: Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)

  • ↑ DO — Maximize Dream Outcome: Not "more bookings" — "your inquiry-to-booking rate at 40%+ in 90 days from leads that are currently going cold."
  • ↑ LA — Maximize Likelihood: Fleur De Lis — 3 bookings, 6 weeks, cold leads. Named. Numbered. Specific proof raises perceived probability.
  • ↓ TD — Minimize Time Delay: "You will see results within 6 weeks of launch." Speed of first result is a value multiplier.
  • ↓ ES — Minimize Effort: "We handle everything — the shoot, processing, embed, and follow-up automation." Done-for-you commands a premium.

DPP Sequence — Dream → Proof → Plan: This is the order that creates belief without resistance. Engage emotion first, then proof, then plan.

"Imagine a prospect touring your space at 11pm from their couch — and booking by Thursday morning. Before you've made a single call."Dream First — engage emotion before logic can form objections

Revenue Compounding
The SEED Referral Engine
The missing delta — engineered referrals

Referrals are not a side effect of good work. They are a system you architect deliberately. Every client is a potential referral gateway. SEED is the four-stage protocol that turns every client relationship into a repeating revenue source.

  • S — Set the Expectation Early: In the first meeting, plant the seed: "Part of how we measure success is when clients refer others."
  • E — Engineer the Proof Moment: At the 6-week mark, show them the data. "You've had X bookings from previously cold leads. Who else needs to see this?"
  • E — Earn Case Study Permission: "Would you be open to us sharing what we built together? It positions you as the operator who figured this out first."
  • D — Deploy Warm Introduction: Give them a specific name — not "anyone you know." Write the introduction message for them. One click to refer.

Second-order insight: every referral client is a higher-trust close. Shorter sales cycle, less resistance, stronger commitment. The SEED engine systematically lowers acquisition cost while raising client quality.
